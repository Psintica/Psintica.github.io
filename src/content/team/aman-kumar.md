Aman Kumar is the founder of Psintica. Driven by a passion for open-source science and STEM mentorship, he established Psintica to bridge the gap between traditional undergraduate education and active scientific research. 

His research interests lie at the intersection of computational astrophysics, deep learning, and open-science frameworks. He leads the Astronomy Research Group and works on engineering software systems to accelerate scientific workflows.
